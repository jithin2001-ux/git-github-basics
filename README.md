# Online Retail Sales Performance & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes historical online retail transaction data to
understand sales performance, customer activity, product performance,
country-level sales, and time-based sales patterns. Python and Power BI
were used to clean, analyze, visualize, and communicate the findings.

## Problem Statement

Analyze historical online retail transactions to identify sales trends,
high-performing products, customer and country patterns, and
opportunities for improving business performance.

## Dataset Description

The project uses the **Online Retail** dataset from the UCI Machine
Learning Repository.

-   Dataset: Online Retail
-   Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository
-   Period: December 2010 to December 2011
-   Original records: 541,909
-   Original columns: 8
-   Main fields: InvoiceNo, StockCode, Description, Quantity,
    InvoiceDate, UnitPrice, CustomerID, Country
-   The dataset contains transactional records from a UK-based online
    retailer.

## Data Cleaning Process

The following preparation steps were performed:

1.  Inspected the dataset shape, columns, data types, and missing
    values.
2.  Identified duplicate records.
3.  Identified cancelled invoices.
4.  Removed duplicate records.
5.  Removed records with missing product descriptions.
6.  Removed cancelled invoices.
7.  Removed transactions with negative quantities.
8.  Removed records with non-positive unit prices.
9.  Created `SalesAmount` using: `SalesAmount = Quantity × UnitPrice`
10. Saved the cleaned dataset as `online_retail_cleaned.xlsx`.

### Cleaning Results

  Measure                      Result
  ------------------ ----------------
  Original records            541,909
  Cleaned records             524,878
  Records removed              17,031
  Total sales          £10,642,110.80
  Countries                        38
  Unique products               3,922

CustomerID missing values were retained in the main cleaned dataset
because customer identification was not required for the overall sales
calculation.

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Descriptive statistics were calculated for Quantity, UnitPrice, and
SalesAmount.

Important observations:

-   Average quantity per transaction: approximately 10.62 units.
-   Median quantity: 4 units.
-   Average sales amount per transaction: approximately £20.28.
-   Median sales amount: approximately £9.92.
-   The large difference between average and median values indicates the
    presence of some unusually large transactions.
-   Correlation analysis was performed for Quantity, UnitPrice, and
    SalesAmount.
-   IQR-based outlier analysis was used to identify unusually large
    values.
-   Monthly sales were analyzed to identify important sales periods.

### Monthly Sales Findings

-   Highest sales month: **November 2011 --- £1,503,866.78**
-   Lowest sales month: **February 2011 --- £522,545.56**
-   Sales increased substantially from September to November 2011.

## Key Business Insights

1.  **Sales increased strongly toward the end of 2011.**\
    Sales rose substantially from September to November, with November
    recording the highest monthly sales of £1,503,866.78.

2.  **The United Kingdom is the dominant market.**\
    The UK generated by far the highest sales compared with other
    countries. The Netherlands, EIRE, Germany, and France were among the
    other important markets.

3.  **A small group of products contributes significantly to sales.**\
    The Top 10 Products analysis shows that a limited number of products
    generate a significant portion of sales. DOTCOM POSTAGE was the
    highest-performing item by sales in the analysis.

4.  **Most transactions involve relatively small quantities.**\
    The quantity distribution shows that most transactions contain
    relatively small quantities, while a small number of transactions
    are unusually large.

5.  **Sales vary by day of the week.**\
    The Power BI dashboard shows stronger sales on Tuesday and Thursday,
    while Sunday has substantially lower sales.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns for high-sales periods

Sales increase significantly during September-November. The business
should prepare sufficient inventory and logistics capacity before this
period and launch targeted seasonal promotions to capture increased
demand.

### 2. Focus on high-performing products and key markets

The company should maintain sufficient stock of high-performing products
and use them in promotions or bundles. The UK should remain a key market
while targeted marketing strategies can be developed for promising
international markets such as the Netherlands, EIRE, Germany, and
France.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that Online Retail sales are concentrated around
specific periods, products, and markets. Sales increased strongly toward
the end of 2011, the United Kingdom was the dominant market, and a small
number of products contributed substantially to revenue. These findings
can support better inventory planning, marketing campaigns, product
management, and market expansion decisions.
